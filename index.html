Sports Video Generation Evaluation

Instructions

Thank you for participating in this evaluation! We are comparing different methods for motion-conditioned sports video generation. Given a ground truth sports video (basketball or soccer), each method generates a new video based on specified player bounding box trajectories (motion paths).

For each example, you will evaluate two anonymous models (Model A and Model B) in two aspects:

  • Step 1 — Motion Trajectory Following: You will see generated videos with bounding boxes overlaid. These boxes represent the target player trajectories that the generated players should follow. Please judge which model's players better follow the given trajectories.
  • Step 2 — Visual Quality: You will see the same generated videos without bounding boxes. Please judge:
    • Player Appearance: Which model generates more realistic and consistent player appearances?
    • Overall Quality: Which video has better overall visual quality (sharpness, realism, and temporal coherence)?

The Ground Truth video is provided as a reference for the original scene and player movements. Please watch all videos carefully before answering.

Please answer all questions before proceeding.
